Why KryptoPesa

A Kenyan crypto-to-cash service should be legible before it is fast. Here is what the platform is being built around.

Identity-checked accounts

Every account is subject to identity verification before it can transact. Verification status is shown in your dashboard and kept in sync.

Pricing you can read

Live market prices with an indicative USD/KES conversion, so you can see what a figure means before any payout service is enabled.

Built for Kenyan phones

Designed for everyday Android devices and mobile data: light pages, tap targets that fit a thumb, and no layout that needs zooming.

Disclosed, not implied

Fees, limits, reversibility and availability are stated on the page where a feature would be used — including when a feature is switched off.

How it works

Three steps once the service is fully enabled. Steps two and three are partly or entirely unavailable today.

  1. Create and confirm your account

    Sign up with an email address, confirm it, then log in. Account creation is available now.

  2. Complete identity verification

    Verification runs through the configured identity provider. Sandbox sessions are available; production approval depends on provider configuration.

  3. Convert to M-Pesa

    When payouts open, a verified account will be able to request an M-Pesa payout. This is disabled today, so no prompt will reach your phone.

What is available today

KryptoPesa is not yet taking or paying money. This section is the source of truth for the current build.

  • Available: account creation, email confirmation, login and session handling.
  • Available: live market prices for BTC, ETH, SOL and WLD, plus an indicative USD/KES rate.
  • Sandbox only: identity verification, depending on the operator's provider configuration.
  • Not available: M-Pesa deposits, crypto-to-M-Pesa payouts, custody deposit addresses and wallet balances.

No wallet balance, transaction or exchange rate on this site is represented as a live settlement. Where a figure is unavailable we show it as unavailable rather than as zero.
