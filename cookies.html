Cookies and tracking

Essential browser storage may be required for login, security and core functions. External scripts and embedded services can also set cookies or collect device information.

Non-essential analytics and marketing tracking should not run before valid consent where required. This page does not assert that every third-party script is already consent-gated; the production integration must be audited.

Use your browser controls to inspect or clear stored cookies. Contact Email us about tracking or consent withdrawal.
